| File | Content |

## How it was made (tools actually used)

No Blender, Figma, Meshy, Tripo or purchased/third-party anatomical asset was used (Blender is not
installed on the build machine). The heart is generated by code in `scripts/heart-asset/`:

1. `anatomy.ts` — signed-distance-field (SDF) model in centimetres: tapered LV, RV crescent with
   infundibulum, atria with lobulated appendages, aorta with sinuses of Valsalva and three arch
   branches, pulmonary trunk with bifurcation, venae cavae, four pulmonary veins. Chamber cavities are
   subtracted to give real walls; orifices connect cavities across the AV junction; papillary muscles
   and the moderator band are solid tissue inside the cavities; procedural trabeculae (LV apex, RV) and
   pectinate ridges (RA appendage). A minimum-wall constraint prevents cavities from breaking through
   the epicardium. Vessel ends are cut flat so wall thickness is visible.
2. `coronary.ts` — grooves are *derived* from the surface field (where the LV and RV fields meet; where
   ventricles meet atria/great arteries). Epicardial fat is built along the AV sulcus and
   interventricular grooves (lobulated by noise). Arteries (left main, LAD, LCx, RCA, PDA, two
   diagonals, obtuse marginal, acute marginal, conus) and veins (great cardiac, coronary sinus, middle
   cardiac, anterior cardiac) are seated onto the fat/epicardial surface with tapering radii.
3. `surface-nets.ts` — Surface Nets meshing with coarse-grid culling, Newton refinement onto the
   surface, SDF-gradient normals, and winding verification.
4. `build.ts` — per-vertex attributes, two LODs, baseline measurements, manifest and GLB export
   (`glb.ts`, a minimal glTF 2.0 writer). Deterministic: a seeded noise table, no `Math.random`.

Rebuild: `npm run build:asset` (≈ 2.5 min on an Apple M2). `--quick` makes a coarse version.

## Attribute contract (any replacement asset must provide it)

| Attribute | Type | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `POSITION`, `NORMAL` | float32 | centimetres, patient frame |
| `_WEIGHTS` | uint8 normalized vec4 | deformation membership (LV, RV, LA, RA); the remainder anchors vessels |
| `_TISSUE` | uint8 normalized vec4 | x: wall depth 0 epicardium … 1 endocardium; y: epicardial fat; z: tissue kind byte (0 myocardium, 1 great artery, 2 great vein, 3 coronary artery, 4 coronary vein); w: region byte (0 lv, 1 rv, 2 la, 3 ra, 4 aorta, 5 pulmonary_artery, 6 vena_cava, 7 pulmonary_veins, 8 coronary_vessels) |

| `_PROV` | uint8 vec4 | x: provenance code — 0 measured from CT, 1 AI segmentation of this CT, 2 dataset label, 3 atlas fitted to this heart (BodyParts3D, CC BY-SA), 4 illustrative. Optional; absent = measured. (docs/atlas-heart.md) |

Regions 6 (vena_cava) and 7 (pulmonary_veins) carry tissue kind 2 (great vein) when the asset has them: the atlas
continuations of `cor_pipeline/vessels.py`, or AI segmentation for Tier B.

Weights are a soft-max over chamber membership fields, so they vary continuously: deformation can't
tear the surface at region borders. Wall depth lets endocardium and epicardium move differently.

## Manifest fields

`assetId`, `assetVersion`, `source`, `author`, `license`, `attribution`, `redistribution`, `units`,
`axes` (+X patient left, +Y superior, +Z anterior), `anatomicalOrientation`, `bounds`, `provenanceTier`
(A), `files` (URLs, bytes, triangle counts), `attributes`, `structureToMesh` (canonical IDs → mesh/region),
`deformation` (LV frame: base, axis, septal and anterior directions, cavity base/apex, mean radius/wall;
RV, LA, RA centres; AHA segment convention), `valves` (annulus centre/normal/radius), `papillary`
(base/tip), `coronaryOstia`, `anchors` (label anchor per structure), `baseline` (measured values),
`morphTargets` (none — handles are used), `layers`, `cutaway`, `animationChannels`, `knownLimitations`.

## Deformation handles (runtime)

Implemented in the vertex shader (`src/scene/materials/deformGlsl.ts`) with a TypeScript reference
(`src/scene/animation/deform.ts`) used for picking, label anchors, valve attachment and tests:

- LV: static ED scale (radial endo/epi, long about the base), cycle scale (long about the apex),
  torsion, AHA-17 segmental motion multiplier and wall-thickness offset (interpolated between ring
  centres and around the circumference).
- RV: free wall moves relative to a septal reference radius; long-axis shortening.
- LA/RA: isotropic about their centres.
- Normals are recomputed in the shader from deformed tangent frames.

Valves (`src/scene/geometry/valves.ts`) are rebuilt each frame from the *deformed* annulus, so leaflets
and chordae stay attached. Each annulus point inherits the attributes of its nearest tissue vertex.

## LOD and texture budget by tier

| Tier | Mesh | DPR | Shadows | Compare views |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| high (desktop, ≥ 6 cores, ≥ 6 GB) | low first, then high in the background | 1–2 | contact shadow (rendered once) | up to 4 |
| low (phones, small screens, weak devices) | low only | 1–1.25 | off | 2 |

Comparison always uses the low LOD. There are **no texture files**: micro-relief, fibre striation, fat
lobulation and colour variation are deterministic procedural noise in rest-pose coordinates (they move
with the tissue and never change between sessions). Consequence: no texture seams and no missing
textures, but also no baked normal/roughness maps — documented as a gap relative to the spec.

## Replacing the asset (Tier A → better Tier A, or Tier B)

1. Produce a GLB with meshes `heart_tissue`, `coronary_arteries`, `cardiac_veins` and the attribute
   contract above (weights/depth/region can be computed by projecting a labelled anatomical mesh onto
   chamber fields — the builder's `vertexAttributes` shows how).
2. Write a manifest with the same fields, including measured `baseline` values and LV frame.
3. Point `src/scene/asset-adapters/heartAsset.ts` at the new manifest and regenerate
   `tests/fixtures/lv-cavity-proxy.json` from the new LV cavity; run `npm test`.
4. Re-run the visual review (docs/anatomy-review.md) — realism must be re-verified, not assumed.

## Tier B: CT-derived assets (implemented for bundled open-data cases)

`pipeline/cor_pipeline/asset.py` builds a GLB + manifest in this contract from a CT label volume
(`provenanceTier: "B"`, plus `center`, `provenance`, `valves.*.fromImaging` and `structureToMesh.*.absent`
for structures the labels lack). `publish.py` copies them into `public/models/ct/` and
`src/scene/asset-adapters/ct/`; `src/scene/asset-adapters/registry.ts` lists every asset with its own
reference profile (its rest pose is the baseline) and capabilities. A record selects one with
`imaging.assets[]: { kind: "mesh", reference: "<assetId>" }`; anything else gets the procedural heart.
Coronary meshes are optional. Everything that used to import the procedural manifest now takes the
asset spec (deformation rest params, valve/flow rigs, camera presets and cut planes, label anchors).

Not supported: uploading a patient's own mesh or images in the app. A user-supplied mesh would still
need validation of units, orientation, labels, provenance and de-identification first.
